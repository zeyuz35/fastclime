## 2026-06-05 - Replaced Informal Language in Documentation

**Learning:** The documentation and roxygen comments contained the informal word "huge" (e.g., "When the dimension is huge..."). This violates professional documentation style. Also, regenerating man pages via roxygen2 requires manual updates to `man/*.Rd` if they were not originally generated by roxygen2.
**Action:** Replaced "huge" with "large" in `R/fastclime.R` and manually in `man/fastclime.Rd` to ensure professional tone.
